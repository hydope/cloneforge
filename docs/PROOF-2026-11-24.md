# Measured proof — 2026-11-24 (EST), CPU-only VM, Python 3.12

Environment: Piper 1.x (amy base voice; lessac + ryan synthetic reference
speakers), faster-whisper tiny (local ASR, av pinned >=12,<17), YourTTS +
XTTS-v2 via coqui-tts 0.28.0 fork in .venv-yourtts (torch 2.11.0+cpu,
torchcodec 0.17.0 from the PyTorch CPU index — see docs/INSTALL.md),
resemblyzer VoiceEncoder (GE2E, 256-dim) in the same side venv, called
from the bench by subprocess. Both reference speakers are SYNTHETIC
(Piper voices) — no real person's voice was used.

Corpus: 3 lines × 2 speakers.
- L1 short/neutral (13 words): “The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog near the river bank.”
- L2 long/formal (27 words): “On Tuesday morning, the committee announced that the new library will open its doors to every student in the district, free of charge, for the entire summer.”
- L3 emotional (19 words): “I cannot believe you forgot her birthday again — she waited by the phone all evening, hoping you would call!”
- Speaker A: en_US-lessac-medium (female-register, ref F0 179–202 Hz)
- Speaker B: en_US-ryan-medium (male-register, ref F0 132–145 Hz)
